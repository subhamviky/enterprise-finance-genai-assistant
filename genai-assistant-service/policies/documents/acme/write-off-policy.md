# Acme Retail: write-off and refund limits

Synthetic document for the demonstration landscape. Not real policy.

## Limits

A write-off may not exceed 250.00 EUR and a refund may not exceed 1000.00 EUR. Amounts are in euros only. An adjustment above its limit is not offered for approval.

## Approval

One finance-approver, other than the person who asked, approves each adjustment. A proposal expires after 24 hours.
