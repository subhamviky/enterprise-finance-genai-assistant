# Globex Logistics: write-off and refund limits

Synthetic document for the demonstration landscape. Not real policy.

## Limits

A write-off may not exceed 1000.00 USD and a refund may not exceed 5000.00 USD. Amounts are in US dollars only. An adjustment above its limit is not offered for approval.

## Approval

Two different controllers, neither of them the person who asked, approve each adjustment. A proposal expires after 48 hours.
