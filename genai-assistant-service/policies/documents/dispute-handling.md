# Disputes and chargebacks

Synthetic document for the demonstration landscape. Not real policy.

## Opening a dispute

A payer may dispute a settlement within 30 days of its date. The dispute holds the settlement: no adjustment is proposed on a settlement while a dispute is open.

## Resolving a dispute

A dispute is resolved by the finance team within 10 business days. The outcome is either the settlement stands, or it is reversed by a refund that follows the normal approval rules.
